  {{< bible ref="Galatians 6:2" emphasize="2" >}}

  - I cannot shoulder every weight alone - bearing means showing up with hands on the load, not commentary from the sidewalk.
  - Level 1: Like when one kid holds the door while another drags the cooler - the job moves because two backs share it.
  - Level 2: Bearing is active help with what crushes someone - not advice from a distance while they still carry it alone.
  - Level 3: Under [[Free Grace]], I am already accepted - I bear burdens from love, not to earn standing or look holy.
  - Level 4: Galatians pairs bearing with restoring the fallen gently - load-sharing and repair belong in the same breath.
  - Level 5: [[Servant Leadership]] names the posture; [[Share the Heavy Lift]] is the workplace echo - same move, different room.
  - [[Love Your Neighbor]] shows up when I trade observation for shared weight.
  - [[Standing vs Fellowship]] keeps my place secure while I help someone whose fellowship lane is cracked.
