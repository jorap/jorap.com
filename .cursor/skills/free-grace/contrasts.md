# Free Grace — contrasts (Anderson only)

How Anderson's Free Grace differs from neighboring systems. Use when auditing a note or answering "isn't that Calvinism / Arminian / Catholic / lordship?" Doctrine only — no other authority.

## Shared words, different parking lots

All major streams say "grace." Free Grace insists grace is **free** — destiny by faith alone; works for walk and rewards.

| Issue | Catholic (Augustinian inheritance) | Dortian Calvinist | Arminian (loss type) | Free Grace (Anderson) |
|-------|------------------------------------|-------------------|----------------------|------------------------|
| Justification | Lifelong process; infused; hope + fear | Instant forensic, but remarried to guaranteed progressive sanctification | Often moral reshaping; can forfeit | Instant forensic declaration; faith alone |
| Perseverance | Required for final acceptance | Elect will; no perseverance → never elect | Believer can lose salvation | Destiny secure; walk may fail; rewards/fellowship at risk |
| Assurance before death | Denied (Trent: no certainty of faith) | Claimed, but fruit/perseverance gut it | Impossible if loss is real | Yes — promises of God |
| Mat 24:13 | Endure → eternal salvation | Same spiritual reading | Same spiritual reading | Physical / end-of-age deliverance in premill frame |
| Faith | Formed by love / sacraments | Saving faith perseveres; temporary faith for reprobate | Faith includes obedience for many | Trust in Christ; obedience is fruit of walk, not essence of justifying faith |

Anderson: Catholics, Calvinists, and Arminians who make perseverance-to-death the ultimate heaven-test all park in the **Vatican lot** — different stickers, same meter.

## Lordship salvation

| Lordship tends to say | Free Grace (Anderson) says |
|----------------------|----------------------------|
| Repentance = turn from sin as condition for justification | Repentance for fellowship; faith for life |
| Faith includes commitment / obedience | Faith is reliance; obedience follows (or fails) after |
| No fruit → not saved | Possible carnal / disciplined believer; loss of reward; or never believed — don't make fruit the *ground* of knowing |
| Kurios = Mastery as entry | Kurios = deity in gospel confession; mastery in discipleship |

Agree with lordship: regeneration matters; antinomian "I prayed once, sin freely" is false. Disagree on **where** surrender sits in the ordo.

## Neo-Reformed / "future justification by works"

Present verdict + future confirmation by works; or faith+works for eternal life.

Anderson Free Grace: that returns assurance to the Final Day and functionally overlaps Catholic process. Present justification by faith alone stands; Judgment Seat assesses **works for reward**, not a second justification that might cancel the first.

## Hard Reformed package (TULIP as airtight chain)

| Point (classic Dortian) | Free Grace pressure (Anderson) |
|-------------------------|--------------------------------|
| Total inability = corpse with zero response | Depraved but responsive under drawing; not automaton |
| Unconditional election as double predestination | Soft cooperative; reject God as author of evil |
| Limited atonement | Sufficient for all; efficient for believers |
| Irresistible = drag | Draw / persuade; preferential will can be resisted |
| Perseverance = prove election | Security of the gift; perseverance not the litmus for destiny |

One broken link (especially limited atonement or perseverance-as-proof) collapses the airtight Dortian chain — Anderson's point. Do not paste "four-point Calvinist" labels without defining terms; Chafer-style redefinitions confuse communication.

## Universalism

| Universalist claim | Free Grace reply (Anderson) |
|--------------------|----------------------------|
| Love wins; all eventually reconciled | Love allows real refusal; robots don't love |
| Forever ≠ forever (`aiōn`) | Parallel "eternal life / eternal punishment"; Rev 20:10 |
| Beast/false prophet = systems | Text treats them as persons; alive in the lake |
| Post-mortem second chance | Appointed once to die, then judgment (Heb 9:27) |

Reacting against Augustinian double-predestination cruelty is understandable; swinging to forced universal reconciliation is not Anderson's fix. Premillennial hope (many born in the Millennium under Christ's reign) softens the "90% tortured forever by decree" portrait without universalism.

## Cheap grace smear

| Charge | Reply (Anderson) |
|--------|------------------|
| Free Grace = license | Grace cost God everything; security frees for grateful obedience, not fear-performance |
| No motive for holiness | Love, fellowship, Spirit, discipline, rewards, loss of reward, Judgment Seat |
| Ignores James / Hebrews | Reads them as brethren, temporal judgment, rewards, inactive faith — not hell for every stumble |

## Compliance use
