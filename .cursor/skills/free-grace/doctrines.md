# Free Grace — doctrine cards (Anderson only)

Compact teaching map from David R. Anderson, *Free Grace Soteriology*, 3rd ed. Attribute; do not paste book text. No other soteriological authority.

## Free gift (grace)

- Eph 2:8–10; Rom 5:15–17; John 4:10; Rev 21:6; 22:17 (`dōrea` / freely)
- Gift ≠ wage (front strings) ≠ bribe (back strings)
- "Free Grace" is biblical emphasis, not redundancy for show

## Depravity and response

- Thoroughly fallen (body, mind, will, spiritual death as separation) — not as bad as possible yet
- Unregenerate can receive natural revelation and be held accountable (Rom 1); Spirit convicts via conscience (John 16; Rom 2)
- Cannot reach God alone; Father draws / persuades (John 6:44, 65; Acts 17:27) — wooing, not dragging as the only lexical option
- Light received → more light (Matt 13 principle); Name of Jesus required when clearly revealed (Acts 4:12)

## OT salvation

- One way of justification across ages: faith in God; means = grace; basis = Christ's blood (retroactive and proactive)
- Content of faith varies with progressive revelation
- OT focus often national / land / fellowship under covenant; do not invent a second way of earning heaven by Law
- Messiah hope present (Gen 3:15; Abrahamic promise; royal psalms; Heb 11:26)

## Cross / atonement

- Substitute, propitiation (God's wrath satisfied by God in Christ), redemption/ransom, reconciliation/peace, positional sanctification at the cross
- Extent: sufficient for the world; efficient for believers (1 John 2:2; 2 Pet 2:1 as pressure against limited atonement)
- Primary theocentric aim includes vindicating God's justice and love — not only "elect headcount"

## Justification

- Declare righteous (forensic), not make righteous as lifelong process
- Instantaneous; alien / imputed righteousness of Christ; *simul iustus et peccator* (position vs condition)
- Faith alone — not faith + Spirit-powered works as the entrance price
- Eschatological blessing already enjoyed (Rom 5:1, 9) — Judgment Seat for believers' works, Great White Throne for the lost

## Ordo salutis (Anderson Free Grace)

Typical order for adults: calling / persuasion → faith → regeneration + justification → (repentance for fellowship as needed) → progressive sanctification → glorification.

Reject: regeneration temporally/logically before faith as the only Reformed necessity; baptismal regeneration as the start of the chain for infants as *the* gospel.

## Repentance

- Internal resolve to turn from sin (more than "change mind" alone; less than observable forsaking as the *definition*)
- For all people; condition for fellowship / sanctifying walk, not for receiving eternal life
- Israel's national repentance / John's baptism: covenant people, temporal wrath, Spirit promise — dual ministry (nation + individuals)

## Faith

- Trust / conviction / appropriation — Reformers' passive receptivity; not Puritan "faith = obedience"
- No NT category of "faith in Jesus as Savior that fails to save"
- Levels of intimacy/fellowship differ (John 2:23 believers not yet entrusted with deeper disclosure) — not "unregenerate sign-faith"
- John: *pisteuō eis* = personal trust unto life

## Eternal security

- Once saved, always saved — grounded in Father's power and love, Son's finished work and advocacy, Spirit's seal and permanent indwelling
- Relationship cannot be undone; fellowship can
- Problem texts: usually fellowship, discipleship/rewards, temporal judgment, figurative language — not revocation of the gift (Rom 11:29)

## Assurance

- Possible before death; of the essence of resting on the promise
- Primary ground: God's witness / Word (1 John 5:9–13), not fruit perfection
- Works may corroborate; they cannot ground — tomorrow's fall would falsify today's "proof"
- Augustine / later Calvin / Puritan systems that require perseverance to death for knowledge of election = no real assurance in life

## Regeneration

- New birth at faith; Spirit and Word; not a years-long process and not "must precede faith" as temporal priority
- 1 John 5:1: adjectival "whoever believes" ≠ "regeneration caused believing" via perfect-tense grammar tricks
- Infant / heathen: God's character (love + justice); age of accountability; vitium without treating every infant as damned reatus (Anderson vs Augustinianism) — blood of Christ still the basis; do not invent a second gospel

## Lordship salvation

- Agree: regeneration changes a person; sin is serious; transformation is expected
- Disagree: total surrender / obedience as *essence* of saving faith; fruit as primary assurance; perseverance-to-death as knowledge of election
- Romans pattern: justified (1–4) → emancipated (5–8) → present/dedicate (6, 12). "Dedication before emancipation is incarceration"

## Sovereignty and responsibility

- Soft / cooperative determinism: God initiates, draws, enables; humans really respond; faith is not a wage
- Reject hard determinism (God efficient cause of evil; robots) and Pelagian autosoterism
- Preferential will vs decreed will (e.g. 1 Tim 2:4 *thelō*); mystery without contradiction

## Universalism

- Reject. Rev 19:20; 20:10 — beast, false prophet, devil tormented forever; do not demote them to "institutions"
- "All" reconciliation texts (Eph 1; Col 1; 1 Cor 15:22) do not override clear everlasting punishment texts
- Preferential desire for all to be saved ≠ forced universal reconciliation

## Hermeneutic: *sōzō* / *sōtēria*

- Lexicon by context: spiritual eternal life; physical rescue; healing; deliverance from Sin Nature (Rom 1 wrath / Rom 5:9–10); inheritance "saved" at Judgment Seat (James)
- Hermeneutical circle: if one part (e.g. Rom 10:9–10 confession as works-price of heaven) breaks the whole (Eph 2), revise the "whole" reading of *saved* in that context
- James 1–5: "brethren" already birthed; "save the soul/life" in walk / Judgment Seat frame; dead faith = inactive
