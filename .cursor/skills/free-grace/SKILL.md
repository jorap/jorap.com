# Free Grace (Anderson only)

**Sole source:** David R. Anderson, *Free Grace Soteriology*, 3rd ed. (Grace Theology Press, 2018).

Encode Anderson's positions. Do **not** paste book prose. Do **not** blend in other soteriological systems, garden habits, or pastoral softens that contradict Anderson. If JoRap voice skills are also loaded, they govern *wording*; this skill governs *doctrine*. When doctrine and habit conflict, **Anderson wins**.

Detail cards: [doctrines.md](doctrines.md). System contrasts: [contrasts.md](contrasts.md).

## Core claim (Anderson)

Eternal life is God's **free gift** received by **faith in Christ alone** (Eph 2:8–10; John 5:24; 1 John 5:13). "Free" means no strings on the front end (that would be wages) and no strings on the back end (that would be a bribe). Obedience, lifelong repentance, and perseverance are the **thank-you life** after the gift — not the ticket into heaven.

Free Grace fits **premillennial** judgment: destiny is settled by faith or unbelief; works are judged for **rewards**, not for re-deciding destiny.

## Non-negotiable distinctions

Mixing these is how Free Grace collapses into Catholicism, Calvinist fruit-inspecting, or Arminian loss-of-salvation.

| Category | Settled by | About |
|----------|------------|--------|
| **Relationship / standing / justification** | Faith alone, once | Forever family; declared righteous |
| **Fellowship / walk / sanctification** | Confession, repentance, obedience | Closeness and growth; can break and restore |
| **Discipleship / rewards** | Faithful use after salvation | Judgment Seat; loss of reward ≠ loss of life |
| **Temporal discipline** | Fatherly chastening in time | Not hell; not "never saved" |

Anderson: **A truth** = relationship; **B truth** = fellowship. A B question gets a B answer (e.g. rich young ruler / inheritance) without rewriting justification as works.

**Position vs condition:** in Christ already holy; on earth the walk is still in process. Fruit-inspecting for election destroys assurance.

## Spreadsheet theology

Changing one ingressive doctrine reshapes the system. Augustine shifted from premillennial to amillennial; Matthew 24:13 was re-read as **spiritual** salvation to death instead of **physical** deliverance to the end of the age. Perseverance became the sine qua non for heaven.

**"Vatican parking lot":** Catholics (lifelong justification / no certitude), Calvinists (no perseverance → never elect), and Arminians (no perseverance → lost salvation) all make **faithful endurance to death** the ultimate test for eternity. Anderson rejects that test for destiny. Assurance is possible **now** from God's promises (1 John 5:13), not from surviving the last gasp without a stumble.

Reject as Anderson rejects: temporary faith that looks elect but isn't; phenomenological believers; present fruit as **ground** of election-assurance. Fruit may **corroborate**; it never **grounds** assurance.

## Anderson positions (summary)

- **Saving faith:** trust / reliance / appropriation of the gospel promise in Christ — not obedience, not surrender-as-entry-fee, not perseverance folded into the *definition* of faith.
- **Lord:** in salvation texts primarily **deity** (Yahweh / *kurios*), not "total mastery or you are not saved." Dedication (`paristēmi`) after justification (Rom 6, 12). Emancipation before presentation.
- **Ordo:** faith → regeneration / justification (not regeneration-before-faith as Reformed necessity). God draws / persuades (John 6:44); soft / cooperative determinism; faith is not a wage.
- **Atonement:** sufficient for all; efficient for those who believe. Reject limited atonement as Dortian chain-link to double predestination.
- **Repentance:** for believers and unbelievers; internal resolve to turn from sin. Condition for **fellowship / enjoying** life with God, not for receiving eternal life.
- ***sōzō* / salvation:** context decides — physical rescue, healing, deliverance from Sin Nature's tyranny, inheritance/rewards — not every "saved" = get into heaven.
- **James 2:** "Dead" faith = inactive / once alive, not "fake faith that never saved." Justification by works = **before people**; Gen 15 / Rom 4 = **before God** by faith alone. Near context: Judgment Seat / profit of works.
- **Universalism:** reject (e.g. Rev 20:10 as persons forever).
- **Cheap grace smear:** Free Grace is not license. Sin still brings discipline, broken fellowship, and loss of reward.

## Compliance audit (faith notes)

When the user wants to know if a note **complies** with Anderson, run this checklist against the note's frontmatter and body. Report **pass / fail / unclear** per item with a short quote from the note. Do not grade voice, tone, or wikilink style — only doctrine.

### Fail if the note teaches or implies

1. Faith + works (including "Spirit-empowered works") as the **price** of final justification / heaven
2. Perseverance to death as the **litmus** for election or for knowing one has eternal life
3. Assurance **grounded** primarily in fruit lists, introspection, or "patient continuance in well doing" as Hodge-style proof
4. Lordship: saving faith *is* total surrender / obedience; no salvation without that as essence of faith
5. Mat 24:13 as the single / ultimate condition for going to heaven
6. Every warning passage (Hebrews, etc.) as hell for genuine believers, with no room for temporal judgment / loss of reward / fellowship
7. Regeneration must precede faith (temporal or "logical priority" that functions as temporal)
8. Limited atonement / double predestination as required gospel
9. Universal final reconciliation of all persons (or demoting Rev 20:10 persons to "institutions")
10. Loss of the free gift of eternal life (Arminian revoke) — relationship undone by walk failure
11. James 2 "dead" faith = never-saving / spurious faith as Anderson's reading (Anderson: inactive)
12. Confession with the mouth (Rom 10) as a meritorious work that purchases heaven rather than open identification tied to sanctifying deliverance in Anderson's Romans reading

### Pass signals (Anderson-aligned)

- Eternal life by faith alone; gift language with no front/back strings
- Standing vs fellowship (A/B) kept separate
- Assurance from God's promise / witness (1 John 5:9–13) as primary
- Security of the believer; walk failures → discipline, fellowship loss, reward loss — not hell by default
- Rewards / Judgment Seat distinguished from destiny
- Repentance placed with fellowship / sanctification when eternal life is in view
- Premill / rewards frame when perseverance or Mat 24:13 appears

### Garden phrasing (notes)

Prefer "works answer grace" / "fruit may back up" / "never grounds assurance." Avoid "works prove faith," "fruit confirms belonging," and "dead on inspection" — those read as fruit-grounds-election even when the rest of the note is Free Grace.
